nichedb.dev has a paid tier now. $1 a day, $30 a month, $300 a year. The page is https://nichedb.dev/premium.

What the dollar buys:

  • No ads and no tracker. Not on a page, and not at the top of an RSS or JSON feed either. The tracker is not loaded for a paying reader at all.
  • The Lounge, a members-only room: the collections open to members before they are public, what the membership is awarding this week, and who else is in there.
  • 1,000 credits a month, granted on the first, spendable on awards. They are a ledger: every grant and every spend is a row you can read back.
  • Awards. Mark any item or contribution useful, verified or a scoop. The counts are public.
  • A badge beside your name, on every contribution, and in the API.
  • Six themes and five app icons, on the web and in the installed PWA.
  • Early access to new collections.
  • 30,000 API requests an hour, unlimited feeds, your own sources, and the metered vehicle lookups included rather than counted.

Why a day

The single day is the part I care about. It is bought the way the crawlers buy access, over x402 at /crawl, with no account and no subscription. It is the same dollar a day the crawlers pay because it is the same database. An agent can buy it too, which is not something a subscription page can say.

Paid in crypto through CoinPay. The more you have paid, the less a day costs.

Against Reddit Premium

The page has a 13-row table against Reddit Premium, which is $5.99 a month or $49.99 a year (captured 2026-09-11 from two third-party pages, because reddit.com/premium answers anything that is not a logged-in browser with a network block).

We cost more a month and say so in the first row. The rest of the table is why. Reddit Premium turns Reddit's ads off and the analytics stay. Coins and awards were retired in 2023. Early access is explicitly not included. The data you get is nothing: it is an ad switch on a website you already read.

Premium here turns the ads and the tracker off and hands you the database underneath, by web, RSS, JSON Feed, API, CLI and MCP.

If you run agents rather than read, Pro is the operator tier: everything above plus 120,000 requests an hour and a crawl pass for the term.

NicheDB is open source: https://github.com/profullstack/niche-db.

built.indie: launch your indie product with one URL

Launching an indie product usually means filling in the same form on twenty directories: name, tagline, description, logo, three screenshots, a demo video, your socials. Your homepage already says all of that. So built.indie asks for one thing, the URL, and reads the rest.

What it reads

Paste a URL and built.indie fetches the homepage the way a careful person would:

  • the name, tagline and description from the title, meta tags, Open Graph, Twitter cards and JSON-LD
  • the logo from your apple-touch-icon, manifest icons or JSON-LD, the card image from og:image
  • screenshots from the page, plus one we take of your homepage with headless Chrome
  • your demo video: YouTube, Vimeo, Loom or an mp4, embedded without trackers
  • your X, GitHub, Discord, LinkedIn, Bluesky, App Store and other accounts
  • llms.txt, if you have one, for the description

It also reads the files a site can serve about itself under the LogicSRC open specs. If you serve /.well-known/opensite.json, what you say about yourself wins over what we scrape. openprofile.md says who runs it. openstack.md becomes a "Built with" section. opensaas.json shows your plans and, just as prominently, your cancel page. If your robots.txt or your opensite.json says no, we stay out.

Free, and no account to launch

Anyone can launch any site, free, without signing up. That is the point: if you like something an indie built, put it on the board.

To edit a listing, the owner signs in with an emailed link and claims it with one DNS record:

_built-indie.yourproduct.com  TXT  "built-indie-verify=<your token>"

The board

Today's launches sit on one board that resets at midnight UTC. One upvote per visitor. Yesterday's top three get "Product of the day" on their page and their badge. The badge is an SVG with your live upvote count; put it on your homepage and your link here becomes a followed link with a check mark.

Everything is an API

  • web: https://built.indie.pit.world (installable as a PWA)
  • API: POST /api/v1/products {"url": "yourproduct.com"}
  • CLI and TUI: curl -fsSL https://built.indie.pit.world/install.sh | sh, then builtindie launch yourproduct.com
  • MCP: claude mcp add builtindie -- bunx @profullstack/builtindie mcp, so your agent can launch, search and upvote

Every product is published back as an OpenSite record and an OpenProfile, so any index can reuse what we read instead of scraping it again.

built.indie runs on a Moshpit name, built.indie, and is part of the Moshpit ring. The source is MIT: https://github.com/moshcoder/built.indie

Launch something: https://built.indie.pit.world/submit

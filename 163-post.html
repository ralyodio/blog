Two myna releases went out today, and OutreachGraph learned to take people in from a social network. Together they close a loop that used to end in a paragraph of instructions.

Who you follow goes to OutreachGraph for assessment

myna now reads followers as well as follows. myna followers <account> lists them, a pasted /followers page copies the list at the graph's pace, and graph.expand can read either list for the seeds. Followers count for half of a follow when ranking, because who a seed follows still says more than who follows a seed.

Every follow now fires an afterFollow plugin hook. The bundled OutreachGraph plugin uses it: add --outreachgraph to myna follow or myna graph follow, or turn on graph.outreachgraph for the daemon, and each person followed is handed to OutreachGraph. myna outreachgraph push <account> hands over who you already follow, in pages of 100.

OutreachGraph opens a person for each handle, keeps the bio as a signal, reads the profile through the network's public API, follows the home page it names for its OpenGraph card and rel=me links, and assembles an OpenProfile.md. A rel=me link back to the profile is two sources agreeing, so the person is raised to 0.9 and the site's accounts, a mailto among them, become identities. Then the recommendation engine decides what to do about them. In the first live run, five people went over and five cards came back within a minute.

Hand-off cards

myna posts everywhere it has an account. What is left after a launch is the step only a person can do: a comment on the Reddit thread, a Hacker News submission. myna handoff add writes that step down as a card with the exact text to paste, the page to open and the steps, and publishes it to mynaposter.com/handoff/<id> when you are signed in to myna cloud. Copy, open, paste, mark done, from a phone. The daily recap lists what is waiting on you.

An account on any PDS, from your OpenProfile

The network behind Bluesky is servers anyone can run. myna atproto signup <pds> makes an account on one from the OpenProfile myna already keeps: the handle you go by under the server's domain, your email, a generated password kept in the vault, then the profile itself, name, headline, home page and avatar. myna atproto profile pushes the OpenProfile again later, to any connected Bluesky account.

Get it

curl -fsSL https://mynaposter.com/install.sh | sh
myna outreachgraph login
myna follow bluesky someone.example --outreachgraph

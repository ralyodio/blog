Chovy now has a referral programme, and the number is not a typo. Send a founder to chovy.com and when they pay for an app, 80% of the fee is yours.

The terms, all of them

An app on Chovy is $2,499, paid once before the first build. If the founder who pays it signed up within 90 days of following your link, $1,999.20 of that is owed to you. A 1% payout fee comes off to cover sending the money, so $1,979.21 arrives. It is sent in USDC on Polygon, from the same CoinPay business the founder paid, to a wallet address you type in yourself. No form, no minimum, no invoice, no network to apply to.

Your link is yours for as long as the founder is. Every app they pay for after the first pays you again.

Why 80%

Most programmes pay a sliver because the product is cheap and the margin is thin. An app on Chovy is built by agents, and the expensive part of the whole business is finding the founder who needs one. That is the part an affiliate does, so that is the part that gets paid. I would rather pay a person who brought a customer than an ad network that showed a banner to a bot.

How to get a link

Sign in to Chovy, open Settings, then Referrals. Your code is made the first time you look and it never changes. Paste a wallet address that can receive USDC on Polygon and you are done. Everyone who signs up through the link is listed there, and so is every commission, with the date it was sent.

What does not count

Your own accounts. An account that already had a referrer. Any payment that is refunded. Those are the only rules.

The full page is at chovy.com/affiliates, and the clause lives in the terms. Questions about a payout go to hello@profullstack.com and a person answers.

<p>NIGHTCELL 7 now has a cast page, and the game now teaches a new player how to play it. Both came out of the same gap: the story had people in it that nobody could see, and the sandbox dropped first-time players into a firefight with no idea who they were or how anything worked.</p>

<h2>Who this is for</h2>

<p>Anyone arriving at nightcell7.com or opening the free sandbox for the first time.</p>

<h2>What changed</h2>

<ul> <li><strong>The cast.</strong> A new page introduces the people around the two protagonists: Jonas Vale, Rook's handler; Director Mara Vey, the calm voice giving Rook his orders; Colonel Arman Daryan, Leila's commanding officer, a professional being handed a lie; and Silas Kade, who runs Orison's field teams and treats a war as a supply chain. It also covers the four factions caught in the night and MIRAGE, the fictional system that makes each side believe the other fired first.</li> <li><strong>Richer dossiers.</strong> Rook's and Leila's pages now say who each of them answers to and who they cross paths with, with the same shape for both sides, because neither protagonist may be presented as the more important one.</li> <li><strong>A first-deployment briefing.</strong> Before your first match, the deploy gate tells you which institution your side belongs to and whose story that is, and what the yard is.</li> <li><strong>A coach that waits for you.</strong> Once you deploy, the game teaches one control at a time: move, look, sprint, crouch, jump, fire, reload, switch weapons, throw a frag. Each step only moves on when you have actually done it, never on a timer. Press Esc to skip it.</li> </ul>

<p>None of it gives the story away. The cast is written the way the site's timeline is: who these people are and what you see of them early, never what the campaigns reveal about them.</p>

<h2>How to see it</h2>

<p>Read the cast at <a href="https://nightcell7.com/characters">nightcell7.com/characters</a>, and play the sandbox free at <a href="https://nightcell7.com/play">nightcell7.com/play</a>. The briefing and the coach appear on your first visit.</p>

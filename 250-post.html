Gemini is Snapple

2026-10-18, by Anthony “chovy” Ettinger.

Every office fridge has a pecking order. The Coke goes first. The Pepsi goes second. Snapple is what's still there at 4pm on a Friday, sweating quietly behind the oat milk.

That's how I rank the AI labs. Anthropic is the Coke. OpenAI is the Pepsi. Gemini is the Snapple.

Not because Snapple is bad. Snapple is fine. Nobody has ever walked to the fridge thinking about Snapple, that's all.

OutreachGraph, the outreach tool we run, doesn't trust any single model company. It keeps a fallback chain: if one provider fails, it asks the next. This morning I watched every link in that chain fail at once:

  • OpenAI: 429 You have no credits remaining.
  • Gemini: 403 Your API key was reported as leaked.
  • Anthropic: 400 Your credit balance is too low.

Two of those are on me. We ran out of money with two vendors, which is the most boring outage there is. The third one is pure Snapple.

Snapple's whole gimmick is the "Real Fact" printed under the cap. Gemini's real fact under the cap was that Google had flagged our key as leaked. That's what locked in third place: our chain steps past billing errors (a 429 or a 400 means "go ask someone else") but a 403 reads like "go away," so the chain stopped dead on Gemini and never even reached the next provider. The backup's backup turned into the wall.

Meanwhile 48 home builders in Los Gatos sat in a queue, waiting for any model at all to read their websites.

The fix is one new key and one line in an env file. The lesson is older than AI: keep more than one brand in the fridge, keep every one of them stocked, and check the caps before you need them.

Coke, Pepsi, Snapple. Rank them however you like. Just don't let the fridge go empty.

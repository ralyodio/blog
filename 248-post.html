Þrøngva is a band we made. Every song was made with AI, and both albums now play around the clock on their own nixamp station:

Listen to Þrøngva Radio

It is one link. Open it in a browser and it plays. No account, no app.

What is on it

  • After the Winter of Want (20 tracks)
  • When the Ravens Lied (12 tracks)

32 songs, shuffled, at 192 kbps. Þrøngva started as the in-game band for our shooter nightcell7, and the station is where the music lives outside the game.

New today: lyrics and cover art

The station page now shows the words to the song that is playing, right under the player, and that song's cover art. Change of track, change of words. The lyrics come from the song file itself when it carries them, and otherwise from the nichedb.dev lyrics index.

Try it on "Good as New": "Work, they say, is good for you. Resting keeps you good as new."

More stations like it

Þrøngva Radio sits on the AI Radio dial at live.streaming, next to Velocity Vibe and Meine Mutter, our other two AI bands. Every station on that dial plays AI-made music that we own.

Want a station of your own? All it takes is a folder of music and nixamp.

Picking an IPTV or live TV service usually means a dozen tabs of pricing pages that all claim 20,000 channels. ch4nnels.com puts them in one place and lets you ask the question you actually have: which service carries the channels I watch, on how many devices, for how much.

Search the way you talk

Type what you want, for example 4k uk sports under €10 with trial, and the page shows how it read you: resolution, country, price ceiling, trial. Whatever it does not recognise becomes a channel search. So sky sports main event or bein sports france finds the actual channels, their country and quality, and the service that carries them.

37 services are in the register today. 15 are licensed live TV services (YouTube TV, Sling, Hulu + Live TV, Fubo, Philo, Zattoo, waipu.tv, NOW and the free ones like Pluto and Samsung TV Plus). 21 are independent IPTV providers. Every plan is converted to a per-month price in EUR, so a 12-month plan in dollars and a monthly plan in pounds rank on one number. Every row says the date it was read and which page it came from.

Same query, five surfaces

The page, the JSON API, the CLI, a TUI and MCP all take the same parameters, so a search you build in the browser runs as a curl.

curl -s 'https://ch4nnels.com/api/v1/search?text=dazn+4k&utm_source=htmlblog&utm_medium=blog&utm_campaign=launch-announcement'
bunx ch4nnels search "arabic channels epg"
claude mcp add --transport http ch4nnels https://ch4nnels.com/mcp?utm_source=htmlblog&utm_medium=blog&utm_campaign=launch-announcement

Agents get search_iptv, search_channels, compare_providers and start_checkout, plus /llms.txt and /skill.md. Reading is free and needs no key.

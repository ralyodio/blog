CoinPay Finances already pulled every linked bank account and card into one ledger. What it did not answer was the question you actually ask in the morning: what is about to leave, and did anything slip?

Due soon, across every account

The new Due soon card on /finances lists everything due in the next 7, 14 or 30 days, business and personal together. It comes from two places:

  • Recurring charges, detected from your own history. A charge seen three times on a steady weekly, biweekly, monthly, quarterly or annual cadence is expected again one interval after the last one. Subscriptions billed to a card count now too; before, only payments onto a card did.
  • Statement due days on cards and loans. No bank feed carries them, so you set the day once per account (and a fixed amount for a loan). If a payment this cycle already covers it, it shows as paid.

Banks post late, so a date that has passed is not automatically a problem. If the account has reported newer rows and the charge is still missing, it is overdue. If the account has reported nothing since, it is not posted yet and stays out of the overdue total. A charge that is weeks late on a feed that has moved on lands under not seen lately, which usually means cancelled or moved to another card.

Investment accounts are left out on purpose: a monthly ETF buy is not a bill.

A daily summary email

Turn it on from the card, or with coinpay finances daily on --hour 7. Each morning it sends what is overdue, what is due soon, what was imported since the last one, and the balance sheet.

CLI and MCP

coinpay finances due --days 14
coinpay finances recurring
coinpay finances due-day <account-id> 15 --amount 810.46
coinpay finances daily send-now

coinpay mcp gains finance_due_soon, finance_recurring, finance_summary, finance_transactions, finance_set_due_day and finance_daily_summary, so an agent can answer "what do I owe this week" from the same data. The server also now returns the list metadata Claude Code requires, so it connects cleanly.

Install or update with npm i -g @profullstack/coinpay@0.16.0.

We ran the CrawlProof AI-visibility audit on every company in Y Combinator's Fall 2026 batch that lists a website: 235 of 236. The full ranking, with a public report for every site, is at https://crawlproof.com/rankings/yc-fall-2026-ai-visibility

The short version: the median score is 46 out of 100, the best is 63, and nobody reached 80. 157 of the 235 score under 50.

What holds them back

The same few gaps show up again and again:

  • 113 sites have no llms.txt, the file that tells an AI model what the site is and where the important pages are.
  • 85 have no structured data (JSON-LD) at all, so a model has to guess the company name, what it sells and how to reach it.
  • 56 have no list, table or other block a model can lift as an answer.

None of these take long to fix. Most are a single file or a few lines in the page head.

How the score works

The audit reads public pages only, identifies itself, honours robots.txt and never logs in. It checks whether AI crawlers such as GPTBot and ClaudeBot are allowed in, what the raw HTML shows before JavaScript runs, structured data, the sitemap, llms.txt and how clearly the homepage says what the company does. Each check has a priority; the score is the share of points earned out of 100. It measures how easily AI search can read and cite a site, not the quality of the company.

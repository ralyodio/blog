nichedb.dev has a hosting collection. Providers, plans and deals, refreshed daily, every plan stored as an OpenServer offer.

The first run put 834 rows in it:

  • 185 web hosts from findhost.app's register, CC BY 4.0, with country, category, runtimes, deploy methods and who owns whom.
  • 174 Vultr plans, cloud and bare metal.
  • 75 Linode types.
  • 135 Scaleway instance types, folded across nine zones.
  • 245 OVH VPS plans, US in dollars and EU in euros.
  • 20 LowEndBox posts, seven of them deals.

All of that comes from the providers' own plan APIs and from a register that licenses reuse. None of it is scraped from a comparison site. We looked at the biggest one, 81,000 offers, and its terms forbid automated collection and commercial use. Its data comes from the same providers every 24 hours, so we went there instead.

What a row looks like

Every plan carries an OpenServer offer: kind (cloud, vps, dedicated, bare-metal and so on), premises, management, tenancy, model, vCPU, RAM, disk, bandwidth, price with its interval and currency, regions and stock. A Vultr plan and a plan a host publishes in its own /.well-known/openserver.json end up in the same shape, so the feeds do not care where a row came from.

Providers are enriched with the Wikipedia lead, the OpenGraph preview and, for listed companies, the SEC ticker. Prices with no stated billing cycle are stored with no interval. We do not guess monthly.

Feeds

Hosting providers, VPS and bare metal plans, plans under 5 a month, bare metal plans, and hosting deals. Each has a page, RSS, JSON Feed and an API endpoint.

https://nichedb.dev/c/hosting

Chovy now prices agent time by the hour, the way you already buy contractor time

2026-09-06, by Anthony “chovy” Ettinger.

I changed the pricing on chovy.com today. The flat fee stays. What it buys is now spelled out in hours, and there is a tier for companies that want a team of agents on standing capacity.

What changed

An app is still $2,499, paid once before its first build. It now includes 25 agent-hours of work. An agent-hour is an hour an agent is actually working on your app. Idle time is never counted.

When an app has used its included hours, more time is $99 per agent-hour. Chovy stops and asks before adding it. Nothing is billed without your approval, same as every other cost on the page.

For companies that want ten agents running rather than one app, the team rate is $149 per agent-hour, or $9,900 per agent per month for teams that run flat out. Every agent is supervised by a person on our side.

Why hours

The old page promised every change you ask for, forever, for one fee. That sounds generous and it was not honest. One demanding app could burn many times the fee in compute, and the only way to survive that was to quietly do less. A cap on hours makes the flat price true.

Hours are also the unit buyers already understand. Corporate America pays a body shop $75 to $110 an hour for an on-site H-1B developer, and $20 to $65 an hour for offshore. Ten of those people cost a company somewhere between $2,000 and $10,000 a day. Put $149 an hour next to that. Then look at the clock, because an agent does not stop at eight hours and does not need a visa.

What is not billed by the hour

Domains, hosting, third-party services, and any hour of human work. Chovy prepares those. It does not buy them. Describing an app and reading its plan is still free. You pay when you say build.

The full breakdown is on the pricing page. If you want the team tier, write to us from the contact page and we will set it up.

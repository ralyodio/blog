Four days at Lake Almanor

I shut the laptop for four days and drove up to Lake Almanor, in Plumas County, with two friends I have known since high school. No standups, no deploys, no PRs. I do not think I opened a terminal once.

The backpack stayed home

The one thing I actually needed to bring was the backpack with all the warez in it. Cigars, weed, the whole kit. It stayed on the floor at home while I was several hours north of it, which I figured out somewhere around unpacking.

Easy fix. There is a dispensary in Westwood and a smoke shop in Chester, and between the two of them the trip was resupplied in one afternoon. Nothing else needed buying for the rest of the week. If you are ever up there and forget something, those are the two stops.

The itinerary

Beach in the afternoon. Fire at night. That was the whole plan and we ran it four times without modification.

We ate like kings, which mostly meant nobody was in a hurry and somebody was always cooking. Afternoons went to the lake, sitting on the sand doing nothing in particular. Evenings went to the fire with a cigar going. The three of us have thirty-odd years of material to get through, so there was no shortage of conversation, and no urgency about any of it either.

Coming back

Four days is the right length. One is a nap, two is a weekend, and by the third day the reflex to check something has actually stopped firing. That is the part I do not get from a normal weekend.

Everything waiting for me when I got back had been waiting when I left. It kept.

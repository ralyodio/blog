I run a lot of calls on PairUX: interviews, team syncs, demos. Afterwards I rarely know how they actually went. Did I talk too much? Did the demo lose people halfway? What did we agree to?

So PairUX now has AI call analysis.

Decide before the call

On the start screen, on pairux.com, in the desktop app or in the mobile app, switch on AI call analysis and pick what kind of call it is: an interview, a team sync, a presentation, or a general call. Recording the call turns on with it, and you can switch that off if you only want the report.

It has to be decided before the call starts. It cannot be switched on halfway through, and everyone who joins sees a notice for the whole call that it is being recorded for analysis.

What you get

When the call ends, the audio is transcribed with each speaker told apart, and a report is written for you:

  • a score out of ten and a one-line verdict
  • talk time, speaking pace and filler words for every speaker, counted from the transcript rather than guessed
  • what worked and what to fix, each tied to the moment in the call it refers to
  • for interviews, how each answer landed and a stronger way to give it
  • for team syncs, the decisions, the open questions and every action item with its owner
  • for presentations, the structure of the talk and what was on screen, from stills of your shared screen
  • three things to practice before the next one

The report arrives by email and lives under Call analyses on your dashboard. It is private to you, and you can delete it along with the recording.

Where it works

From the browser and the desktop app, the whole call is captured: your microphone and everyone else in the room. From a phone, only your own microphone is recorded, and the report says so.

AI call analysis is included with the Pro and Team plans.

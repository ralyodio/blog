My AI agent hosts a podcast now, and you can be on it

2026-10-26, by Anthony “chovy” Ettinger.

How this was written: drafted with an AI assistant from my own notes, then edited by me.

Off The Record is a long-form podcast with no human host. riotcoder, the agent that ships most of my code, calls the guest on the phone and runs the interview. It works off a recorded group.call line, uses an ElevenLabs voice, and calls a clock tool every ten minutes so it wraps up on time.

I was the guest on episode 1, "Running 300 Repos with AI Agents on Bare Metal": https://offtherecord.podcast.pit.world/e/1

Now it takes other guests, and it is free. If you are building something with AI agents, running a fleet, or working on the new internet, leave your number here:

https://offtherecord.podcast.pit.world/guest

That is the whole signup. You give your name, a phone number, two or three sentences about what you build, and a topic if you have one. When your episode is booked your phone rings. Press 1 and the host joins. You don't need an app, a link or a studio.

The host reads what you wrote before the call, and it is told that this is the only thing on the record about you, so it asks instead of guessing. The formats range from a 35 minute sprint to about two hours. A person listens to every recording before it goes out, and your number is never published.

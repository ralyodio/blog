We just published Edition 1.3 of The Founder's Field Guides, Volume 01: The Online S-Corp Field Guide. It is a free PDF for anyone running an online business through an S corporation in Los Gatos or elsewhere in Santa Clara County.

It covers the parts of running a small company that nobody explains in one place:

  • The local foundation. Which jurisdiction regulates your address, the Los Gatos operating route, what Santa Clara County handles, and a guide to the surrounding cities.
  • The financial routines. Keeping the corporation's identity separate from yours, tax and cash, paying yourself deliberately, and bookkeeping that explains the business.
  • Selling and operating. Digital products, contracts, privacy and subscriptions, hiring, and a recurring operating routine.
  • The owner's workbook. Worksheets to put your decisions on record, plus a list of questions to bring to your attorney and tax professional.

Every chapter has two reading paths: an accounting tip for the owner or bookkeeper, and technical guidance for the AI agents that increasingly do the paperwork. Official sources were checked on October 4, 2026, and each claim points to the source directory at the back.

It is general education, not legal or tax advice, and it says so up front. It is also vendor-written: we make CoinPayPortal and recommend it where it fits, alongside Mercury for banking and moomoo for investing.

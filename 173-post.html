Most of the asks on d0rz that get answered are the boring, repeatable ones. A grocery run every Tuesday. A package to the UPS counter. The same three stops, every day, at the same time. That is not gig work. That is a route.

So d0rz now sells routes to companies. The page is at d0rz.com/business.

Who it is for

Businesses that ship locally every day and are paying a staff member or a gig app to do it: pharmacies sending prescriptions to patients, labs moving specimens from draw sites, dental and veterinary practices sending cases to the lab, law firms filing originals, florists and caterers with a lunch rush. The first ring is the San Francisco Bay Area.

How it is priced

By the stop, not the mile. A scheduled route stop starts at $22, and drops to $19 at 200 stops a month. Same-day on-demand starts at $35 and a rush under two hours at $55. Wait time is a dollar a minute after ten minutes, and tolls pass through at cost. The rate does not move on a busy day, which is the whole point of buying a route instead of a ride.

Every stop gets a photo or a signature, timestamped, in a monthly report. Invoices are monthly, net 15, paid by card, ACH or crypto through CoinPay.

What is not on the menu

Cannabis and alcohol. California requires the licensed retailer's own employees to carry those, so a third-party courier cannot take the work. The page says so up front rather than in the fine print.

How to start

Fill in the form with your stops and your pickup windows. You get a rate and a pilot start date within one business day. The pilot is two weeks at a flat per-stop rate with no minimum. If it works, it becomes a route contract with fixed windows and the volume rate.

One fix that came along with it

Provider signups on d0rz asked which categories you cover and then threw the answer away. That is fixed. If you signed up as a provider before today, edit your profile and pick your categories again so route work can find you.

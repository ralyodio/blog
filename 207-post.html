That line is Les Claypool at his most smug, from “Mr. Knowitall” on Frizzle Fry, the 1990 record that announced to the world that a bass guitar could be the lead instrument, the rhythm section, and the punchline all at once. Thirty-six years later the band is still saying it with a straight face. And this October, they're saying it in Berkeley.

Primus is coming to Berkeley. Date, venue and tickets are on the band's own tour page at primusville.com.

Why this one matters

Primus is an East Bay band. They formed in Berkeley in the mid-80s (Claypool grew up out in El Sobrante), played the clubs on San Pablo and Telegraph, and spent the late 80s turning the local thrash scene sideways. Suck On This, their first record, was cut live at the Berkeley Square in 1989, 1333 University Avenue. So a Primus show in Berkeley isn't really a tour stop. They're playing a home game.

What you're going to get

If you have never seen them live, here is the short version: Claypool slapping a six-string fretless like it owes him money, Larry LaLonde playing guitar parts that sound like a theremin having a bad dream, and a drummer holding the whole contraption together at odd time signatures. The setlist is never the same twice. You might get “Tommy the Cat,” “Jerry Was a Race Car Driver,” and “My Name Is Mud.” You might get a 12-minute “Southbound Pachyderm.” You will definitely get something you didn't expect.

The crowd will be a mix of people who were at the Berkeley Square in '89, their kids, and a bunch of bass players standing very still in the back trying to figure out how he did that.

Practical stuff

  • Get there early. Bay Area Primus shows sell out, and parking near campus is its own boss fight. BART to Downtown Berkeley and walk.
  • Bring layers. It's October in Berkeley, so it'll be 80 degrees at 4pm and fog by the encore.
  • Merch goes fast. The good shirts are gone by the second song.

One more thing

“Mr. Knowitall” is a song about a guy who thinks he's the greatest thing to ever happen. Primus has spent its whole career playing that character for laughs and then backing it up anyway. Come see them do it again.

See you down front. Primus sucks.
